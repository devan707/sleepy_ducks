Privacy Policy

Sleepy Ducks — a Mountain Maker Studio game · Last updated August 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Sleepy Ducks collects, why, and how it's used. We built the game to be simple and mostly self-contained — most of what it stores never leaves your device.

Local game data (stays on your device)

Your high scores, personal bests, and settings (music/sound, name shown on the results screen) are saved with your browser's local storage. This data is not transmitted to us and is not tied to an account — clearing your browser storage clears it.

Anonymous gameplay analytics

To understand whether players want more levels, the game may send anonymous gameplay events (for example: a level started, finished, or failed; a button tap) to our analytics provider, PostHog. These events are tied to a random id generated in your browser — not your name, email, or any device fingerprint — and contain no personal information. This lets us count events, not identify people. Analytics can be unavailable or disabled entirely without affecting how the game plays.

Email address (only if you sign up)

If you choose to enter your email to be notified when Level 2 is ready, we store: your email address, the time you signed up, where you signed up (e.g. the Level 2 waitlist screen), and which version of this policy you agreed to. This is kept separate from the anonymous analytics above — your email is never included in an analytics event. We use it only to send you a one-time update about Level 2. We don't sell it, and we don't send any other email at this time.

Want your email removed from the waitlist? Email us at devan.hemmings@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties for marketing
  • No device fingerprinting

Children's privacy

Sleepy Ducks is a general-audience game and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. The optional email waitlist should only be used by someone old enough to consent to receiving an email, or with a parent/guardian's permission.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email devan.hemmings@gmail.com.
